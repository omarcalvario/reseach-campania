# Universidad del Valle de Puebla Campaign Research Constitution

## Core Principles

### I. Useful Editorial Research
The site MUST serve graphic, audiovisual, and web design research for the Universidad del
Valle de Puebla (UVP) 2027 advertising campaign. Pages MUST prioritize visual clarity,
useful hierarchy, and editorial grids, and MUST remain readable on mobile devices.
This keeps the research actionable across creative disciplines and screen sizes.

### II. Traceable Evidence
Every campaign, video, site, or other resource MUST include its source, URL, country,
approximate date, piece type, and a concise learning. The project MUST prioritize official
sources, case studies, agencies, awards, official landing pages and videos, and specialist
media. It MUST NOT invent metrics, outcomes, links, campaigns, or trends. Traceability lets
readers verify claims and distinguish evidence from inference.

### III. Evidence, Interpretation, and UVP Proposal
Content MUST clearly separate verifiable evidence, the project's interpretation, and any
proposal for UVP. Interpretations and proposals MUST NOT be presented as established facts.
This separation protects research integrity while making strategic implications explicit.

### IV. Originality and Responsible Use
The project MUST NOT copy third-party identities, photographs, slogans, or campaigns.
Every external resource MUST retain its credit, link, and a note describing its permitted
or intended use. References inform original work; they are not assets to reproduce.

### V. Visual Direction from Required References
The presentations in `referencias/estilo/` are mandatory sources of visual direction.
Design decisions MUST consider those references while applying typographic hierarchy,
editorial grids, clear modules, and accessible reading. Collage, texture, glass effects,
3D, gradients, and motion MUST be used as purposeful accents, not persistent decoration.

## Accessibility and Experience

The interface MUST provide sufficient contrast, visible keyboard focus, keyboard
navigation, and support for reduced motion. These requirements apply to all pages and
interactive elements, including visual treatments and media controls.

## Research and Editorial Constraints

Research records MUST preserve each resource's provenance and usage note wherever it is
presented. Claims MUST link to verifiable sources. When a date is approximate or a detail
cannot be verified, the content MUST identify that uncertainty rather than imply precision.

## Governance

This constitution governs project specifications, design, content, and implementation.
Proposed amendments MUST be documented and reviewed for their effect on existing
principles and project artifacts. Reviewers MUST check applicable work for compliance
with these principles; any exception MUST be explicit, justified, and approved before it
is treated as a project decision.

Versioning follows semantic versioning: MAJOR for incompatible governance changes,
MINOR for new or materially expanded principles or sections, and PATCH for clarifications
that do not change intent. Every amendment MUST update the version and last-amended date.
Compliance MUST be reviewed during specification and design review, and again before
publication or release. Conflicts are resolved in favor of this constitution unless an
approved amendment changes the governing rule.
